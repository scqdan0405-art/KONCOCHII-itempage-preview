01

Company Establishment and Vietnam Entity Setup Support

Client

Japanese manufacturing company

Background

A Japanese company considering business expansion into Vietnam asked us for support in preparing to establish a local entity.

We reviewed the business purpose and market-entry objectives, then organized the required procedures, preparation items, coordination with local specialists, and the post-establishment operating structure.

Support Provided
  • Organized requirements for company establishment
  • Supported coordination with local specialists
  • Structured the establishment schedule and procedures
  • Supported preparation for accounting, tax, labor, and bank account setup
  • Supported planning for the initial operating structure after establishment

Key Points for Establishing a Vietnam Entity

Company establishment in Vietnam requires prior organization of many items, including business licenses, investor information, legal representative, office address, bank account, and accounting and tax structure.

It is important to prepare not only for the establishment procedure itself, but also for accounting, tax, and labor management after business operations begin.

Koncochii supports the practical work required for company establishment as well as the operating structure needed after setup.

02

Accounting Introduction and Management System Development

Client

Japanese company that started business in Vietnam

Background

After starting business in Vietnam, the client asked us about cooperation with an accounting firm and the development of a management structure for local accounting, tax, and monthly control.

Vietnam has many operations that differ from Japan, including tax filing, invoice management, payroll calculation, and expense processing. It was necessary to build a structure that allowed management figures to be understood accurately from the early stage and reviewed from Japan.

Support Provided
  • Supported coordination with a local accounting firm
  • Organized first meeting topics and confirmation items
  • Designed monthly management items
  • Organized review items for sales, purchases, expenses, personnel costs, and cash flow
  • Supported design of management materials that can be checked from Japan

Key Points for Accounting and Management Systems

When doing business in Vietnam, it is important not only to leave accounting and tax filings to local providers, but also to keep the figures needed for management decisions visible from Japan.

A monthly process for checking sales, costs, expenses, cash flow, accounts receivable, and accounts payable makes it easier to identify issues early.

Koncochii supports not only cooperation with local accounting firms, but also visualization of the numbers needed for business management.

03

Industrial Real Estate and Production Site Candidate Research

Client

Japanese manufacturing company considering production startup in Vietnam

Background

The client wanted to start production in Vietnam without building its own factory, and asked us to research rental factories and serviced factories that could reduce initial investment and enable operations to begin in a short period.

While confirming production scale, required area, power and water conditions, logistics conditions, and investment plans, we researched industrial parks and rental factory candidates mainly in southern Vietnam.

Support Provided
  • Organized business content, production scale, and required area
  • Confirmed power, water supply and drainage, and logistics conditions
  • Researched industrial parks and rental factories around Ho Chi Minh City, Binh Duong, Dong Nai, Long An, and Ba Ria-Vung Tau
  • Compared location, rent, infrastructure, legal conditions, expandability, and attached services
  • Arranged local site visits
  • Coordinated meetings with industrial park operators
  • Supported confirmation of contract terms and factory startup schedule

Key Points for Selecting Industrial Real Estate and Production Sites

When selecting a production site in Vietnam, it is important to review not only rent and location, but also power capacity, water supply and drainage, logistics access, hiring environment, legal conditions, and future expandability.

Rental factories and serviced factories can reduce initial investment and shorten the time to start operations compared with building an owned factory, but contract terms, renovation restrictions, equipment conditions, and startup procedures must be checked carefully.

Koncochii supports not only candidate research for industrial parks and rental factories, but also local site visits, meeting coordination, condition organization, and market-entry scheduling.

This project was completed after selecting candidate factories and industrial parks, supporting local inspections, organizing contract terms, reviewing the expansion plan, and providing the information needed for site selection and market entry.

04

Liquid Transport Packaging and Industrial Packaging Solutions

Client

Company handling liquid products, food ingredients, and industrial liquids

Background

The client asked us about introducing Paper IBC containers to reduce logistics costs, improve storage efficiency, and improve packaging for liquid products.

We reviewed the optimal liquid transport packaging solution by confirming product characteristics, transport method, storage conditions, export destination, and required capacity, while also comparing stainless tanks, drums, plastic IBCs, and flexitanks.

Support Provided
  • Confirmed product characteristics, viscosity, specific gravity, and filling temperature
  • Organized transport method, storage conditions, export destination, and required capacity
  • Proposed Paper IBC and liquid transport containers from 1,000L to 14,000L
  • Confirmed inner bag specifications, pallet structure, filling method, and discharge method
  • Organized storage conditions, transport risks, and introduction precautions
  • Compared loading efficiency, container utilization, logistics costs, and storage efficiency
  • Coordinated manufacturer introduction, logistics improvement proposals, transport testing support, and product supply

Key Points for Liquid Transport Packaging

For liquid product transport, packaging must be selected according to the nature of the liquid, transport distance, storage period, filling and discharge method, and destination requirements.

Paper IBC can be used for many liquid products, including malt syrup, food syrups, liquid caramel, coconut oil, vegetable oil, concentrated juice, liquid food ingredients, fermentation ingredients, non-hazardous industrial liquids, and liquid raw materials for food, beverages, cosmetics, and daily goods.

It is also important to compare existing containers from the perspectives of loading efficiency, storage efficiency, logistics cost reduction, recyclability, and environmental impact.

Koncochii provides integrated support from Paper IBC proposals to manufacturer introduction, logistics improvement proposals, transport testing, comparison with existing containers, and product supply.

This project was completed after confirming transport conditions, selecting containers, preparing logistics simulations and introduction proposals, and submitting packaging improvement and Paper IBC proposal materials.

The target market and selling price affect required quality standards, packaging specifications, and logistics conditions.

Koncochii organizes agricultural production, processing, quality control, cost, and transportation as one plan to support business feasibility decisions.

Precision Machining and Vietnam Production Partner Development

Client

Japanese manufacturing company looking for precision-machined parts suppliers in Vietnam

Background

The client wanted to find suppliers in Vietnam for precision-machined parts, metal parts, CNC-machined parts, jigs, molds, and related products.

At the same time, it was difficult to understand local suppliers' technical capabilities, quality level, delivery response, and inspection systems, and the challenge was how to build a management structure that could meet Japanese quality requirements.

Support Provided
  • Confirmed drawings, specifications, and required quality
  • Confirmed production feasibility
  • Selected suppliers in Vietnam
  • Obtained quotations and organized production conditions
  • Conducted supplier audits and production process checks
  • Managed production progress, dimensional inspection, and visual inspection
  • Confirmed pre-shipment quality and managed quality records
  • Supported regular progress reports and preparation of quality management documents

Key Points for Precision Machining and Production Partner Development

When procuring precision-machined parts in Vietnam, it is important not only to find low-cost suppliers, but also to confirm drawing comprehension, machining accuracy, measurement systems, quality records, delivery control, and improvement capability.

CNC turning, machining center processing, precision metal parts, parts made from drawings, jig production, mold design and production, sheet metal processing, welding, assembly, prototype production, and small-lot production each require different technical capabilities and management systems.

Koncochii supports the entire process beyond supplier introduction, including drawing review, production feasibility confirmation, quotation collection, production startup, process checks, quality inspection, and pre-shipment confirmation.

For projects requiring high quality, we also support preparation of inspection standards, measurement records, quality reports, and other quality management documents to build a production system aligned with Japanese quality expectations.

This project was completed after supplier selection, production startup, quality management, and pre-shipment inspection, ending with delivery of products that met the customer's required specifications.
