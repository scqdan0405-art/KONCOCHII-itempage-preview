Our core services

KONCOCHII offers two services to help you move forward with trading or sales in Japan and make an informed next decision.

Three-Month Advisory

Prepare for expansion into Japan
and define a path forward in three months.

We review your products and business and determine priorities for Japan. Over three months, we help organize pricing, MOQ, lead times, quality, certifications and materials, then clarify the preparation needed to work with Japanese companies and the next steps.

Suitable ifYou are about to start preparing for Japan or are already doing so.
Test Sales Support in Japan

Sell in Japan
and see how the market responds.

We support a test sale through Makuake, a Japanese platform for advance purchases supporting new projects. From preparation and sales-period support to reviewing results, we help assess buying behavior, price response and customer feedback to inform your next decision.

Suitable ifYou have chosen, or have a candidate for, a product to sell in Japan.
